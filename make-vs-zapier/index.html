Make vs Zapier: Choosing an Automation Platform

Make and Zapier both connect apps and automate work. Your best fit depends on workflow complexity, ease of setup, and how usage is billed.

Disclosure: Velcendra may earn a commission when you purchase through certain links on this page, at no additional cost to you. Our comparisons are informational and do not guarantee that any product is right for every business.

Quick answer

Consider Zapier for straightforward, quickly configured app-to-app workflows. Consider Make for visual, multi-step processes that need branching, transformations, and closer inspection of each step.

Workflow building

Try building your real workflow in both products. Check conditional logic, error handling, filters, scheduling, and data transformation instead of judging by template counts.

Integrations

Both offer many integrations, but support for a particular trigger or action varies. Confirm the exact apps and operations your business requires.

Pricing considerations

Do not compare headline subscription prices alone. Estimate runs, tasks or operations, polling frequency, premium app requirements, and error retries for your expected volume.

Who should choose Make?

Teams comfortable mapping complex processes visually and testing multi-step automation should evaluate Make.

Who should choose Zapier?

People seeking quick setup for common business app connections may prefer Zapier's approach.

Questions to ask before buying

How often does the workflow run? How many steps execute per run? What happens on failure? Does your plan include the required integrations? Who will maintain the automation?
