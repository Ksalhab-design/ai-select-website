Quick answer

Automate one well-defined, low-risk repetitive process first. Measure hours saved and monitor errors before expanding to more complex workflows.

Questions to ask before buying

  1. What problem must this software solve in the next 90 days?
  2. What is the full cost for your expected users, transactions, and add-ons?
  3. Does it integrate with the systems you already use?
  4. How difficult is migration if you change providers later?
  5. What support, security, and data-export features do you need?
