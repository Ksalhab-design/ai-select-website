Quick decision guide

Which should you choose?

Choose Zapier if…

You want a fast start, relatively straightforward app connections, and a guided setup experience. It's often the easier first test for a small business new to automation.

Choose Make if…

You need more elaborate workflow logic, visual branching, or frequent data transformation—and you're comfortable investing time in setup.

Neither is universally cheaper. Your actual bill depends on workflow design, usage volume, plan restrictions, and the provider's current pricing.
