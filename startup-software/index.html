Frequently asked questions

What software does a startup need?

Most startups need a way to communicate with customers, track money and deliver their product or service. Website, ecommerce, CRM and automation tools depend on the business model.

Should I buy all my business software immediately?

Usually not. Begin with essential tools, validate demand and add software when a clear need arises.
