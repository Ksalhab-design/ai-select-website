Best E-commerce Platforms: Shopify vs Wix

Compare Shopify and Wix for launching an online store, including fit, trade-offs, and questions to ask before buying.

Editorial overview · October 2026 · Verify current features and pricing directly with providers.

Quick answer

If your primary objective is operating an online store, start by evaluating Shopify. If your priority is a flexible business website with occasional selling, compare Wix. Neither is automatically best for every budget or workflow.

Questions to ask before buying

  1. What problem must this software solve in the next 90 days?
  2. What is the full cost for your expected users, transactions, and add-ons?
  3. Does it integrate with the systems you already use?
  4. How difficult is migration if you change providers later?
  5. What support, security, and data-export features do you need?
