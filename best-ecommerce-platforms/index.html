Shopify vs Wix: Find Your Best Fit

Two capable platforms. Different starting points. Compare the priorities that matter before you commit.

Research-based editorial guide · Reviewed October 24, 2026 · US pricing shown where specified.

The quick answer

If your primary objective is operating an online store, start by evaluating Shopify. If your priority is a flexible business website with occasional selling, compare Wix. Neither is automatically best for every budget or workflow.

Verified pricing

What will it cost?

The numbers below are advertised US plan prices, not a personalized quote. Both headline prices assume annual billing; verify the total due at checkout. Promotions, taxes, billing terms and payment methods can change the total.

Shopify · US

Basic from $29/month

US price with annual billing; $39/month with monthly billing.

Basic: $29/month when billed annually, or $39/month when billed monthly. Grow: $79/month annually or $105/month monthly. Advanced: $299/month annually or $399/month monthly.

Shopify lists standard online card processing on Basic starting at 2.9% + 30¢. Shopify separately lists a 2% third-party transaction fee on Basic for eligible transactions processed through third-party payment providers. That fee is not an additional Shopify fee on standard Shopify Payments card transactions; an external provider may charge its own processing fees.

Source: Shopify official pricing and Shopify payment-fee documentation.

Wix · plan-dependent

Core from $29/month

US advertised price with annual billing paid upfront; confirm checkout price.

Wix offers free website creation, but accepting online payments requires an eligible paid plan. Wix lists Core at $29/month and Business at $39/month when billed annually and paid in full upfront. Taxes, promotions and local checkout prices may differ.

Payment methods and processing costs depend on your country and selected payment provider. Compare your actual processing setup rather than assuming a single universal rate.

Sources: Wix official plans and Wix payment documentation.

Plan prices exclude processing charges and optional apps. A 2.9% + 30¢ fee on a $50 online order would be $1.75, where that published rate applies; do not assume the same rate for Wix or every Shopify plan. Published provider prices reviewed October 24, 2026. Recheck directly before purchase. Affiliate relationships do not change the listed provider prices.

Five questions to ask before buying

  1. What problem must this software solve in the next 90 days?
  2. What is the full cost for your expected users, transactions, and add-ons?
  3. Does it integrate with the systems you already use?
  4. How difficult is migration if you change providers later?
  5. What support, security, and data-export features do you need?
Practical buying example

Estimate your actual cost

For illustration, a Shopify Basic store on annual billing with $5,000 in monthly US online card sales across 100 orders would have a published base subscription of $29 plus approximately $175 in standard card processing (2.9% × $5,000 + 30¢ × 100), or about $204 per month before apps, taxes, refunds, other charges or discounts. This is an example, not a quote or a Wix comparison.

Our editorial recommendation: Prioritize Shopify when running the online store is the central operation. Prioritize Wix when the website, content and presentation come first and selling is a secondary need. If your business requires complex inventory, subscriptions, international payments or POS, compare those exact workflows before committing.
