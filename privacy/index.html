Privacy Policy

Velcendra provides editorial software comparisons and links to third-party providers. This notice explains how Velcendra handles information through its website. Third-party services may have their own privacy practices.

Information and links

The software finder currently processes your selections in your browser to display a recommendation. It does not submit those selections to Velcendra's server through a form. When you follow an external provider or affiliate link, that provider and its tracking partners may process information according to their own policies.

Analytics and cookies

Velcendra offers an optional Google Analytics 4 measurement service. We load the Google Analytics tag only after you select “Accept analytics” in the on-site notice. If you agree, Google may collect page views and usage information, including software-finder selections and clicks to external providers, using cookies and related technologies. We do not intentionally send your name or email address in these analytics events. Google processes this information according to its own terms and privacy practices. You may decline analytics and still use the site.

Your analytics preference is stored in your browser's local storage. To reset your choice, clear this site's local storage/site data in your browser. Hosting providers may separately process technical request data to deliver the website. Affiliate links may involve third-party tracking when you click them, independently of your analytics choice.

Contact

For privacy questions, see our contact page. You can contact us at contact@velcendra.com.
