What Software Do I Need to Start a Business?

When you are starting a business, you may search for new business software, startup tools, software to open a small business or everything needed to run a business. The answer depends on whether you sell services, products or both. Start with a small set of tools that solves real problems, then expand as customers arrive.

Start with your business type

Local service business

A cleaner, consultant, salon or repair company may first need a simple website, a contact or booking form, and a reliable way to follow up with customers. A full online store may not be necessary. See website and ecommerce platform differences and CRM comparisons.

Online shop

Prioritize a product catalog, secure checkout, order tracking, shipping and customer communication. Confirm payment providers, currency support and taxes in your region before subscribing. Compare online store platforms →

Freelancer or solo professional

Begin with a professional web presence, scheduling, basic record keeping and a system for tracking prospective clients. Only add CRM or automation when it improves your workflow. Explore small business software →

Growing startup or team

As work becomes more complex, shared customer records and automation can reduce missed follow-ups and repetitive admin. Compare CRM software and automation tools based on the systems you already use.

A simple software setup checklist

  1. Get found: choose a website or online presence customers can trust.
  2. Get contacted: offer an enquiry form, email or booking method.
  3. Get paid: select payment and invoicing methods supported in your country.
  4. Keep track: organize orders, enquiries and customer follow-ups.
  5. Save time later: automate only the tasks you perform repeatedly.

Do you need all-in-one business software?

Not always. An all-in-one platform can reduce integrations, but it may include features you never use. Separate tools can offer flexibility but add subscriptions and setup work. Compare the total cost, ability to export data and ease of use before deciding.

Questions before choosing

What is the first software I should buy for my business?

Choose the tool that solves your most immediate problem, such as accepting enquiries, publishing a website or taking orders. Many businesses can test a free or entry-level plan first.

Can I start a business using free software?

Some tools offer free plans, but check limits on features, branding, users and data export. See our guide to affordable business software →
