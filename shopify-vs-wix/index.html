Shopify vs Wix: Which Should You Choose?

Both platforms can launch an online store, but the right choice depends on whether selling products or building a broader website is your primary goal.

Disclosure: Velcendra may earn a commission when you purchase through certain links on this page, at no additional cost to you. Our comparisons are informational and do not guarantee that any product is right for every business.

Quick answer

Choose Shopify when your business is primarily an online store and you expect to need deeper commerce workflows. Consider Wix when website design, content, and a smaller store matter equally. Neither is universally better.

Selling and operations

Compare product variants, inventory locations, shipping rules, checkout options, integrations, and point-of-sale needs. Verify the features included in your chosen plan and region before committing.

Design and ease of use

Wix emphasizes flexible visual site building. Shopify centers its platform around commerce. Test each editor with your actual catalog and branding requirements.

Total cost

Compare plan fees, payment-processing charges, transaction fees where applicable, paid apps, domain, email, and the time required to maintain the site. Prices and terms change.

Who should choose Shopify?

Businesses prioritizing ecommerce operations, a growing product catalog, and dedicated selling tools should shortlist Shopify.

Who should choose Wix?

Businesses that primarily need a flexible website with content, appointments, or a modest storefront should shortlist Wix.

Questions to ask before buying

How many products and variants will you sell? Do you need in-person selling? Which payment providers work in your country? How many third-party apps will you pay for? What happens if you migrate later?
