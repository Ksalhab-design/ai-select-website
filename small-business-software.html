What software does a small business need?

Most businesses can start with a website or online store, a way to manage customers, and a few tools to automate repetitive work. You may not need every category immediately. Begin with the tools that solve a real problem today.

How to choose without overspending

  1. Define your immediate goal. Is it building a website, selling online, managing customers, or saving time?
  2. Set a realistic budget. Include upgrades, transaction fees, and additional users—not just introductory pricing.
  3. Check integrations. Make sure the software connects to the tools you already use.
  4. Test before committing. Use demos or trials to assess the actual workflow and ease of use.
  5. Plan for growth. Consider whether you can export data and switch services later.

Where should you start?

If you're launching a business, start with the software category that supports your first customer or sale. If you're already operating, prioritize the bottleneck costing the most time or lost opportunities.
