HubSpot vs Zoho CRM: Find Your Best Fit

Two established CRM platforms, different strengths. Compare how each supports your sales process, team and budget before committing.

Research-based editorial guide · Updated October 24, 2026 · Confirm current plans and features with each provider.

The quick answer

Start with HubSpot if you prioritize a streamlined starting experience and close alignment between sales, marketing and service tools. Start with Zoho CRM if configurable sales workflows and a wider suite of Zoho business applications are especially important. Neither is automatically the lowest-cost option for every team.
Connected growth focus

HubSpot

Worth evaluating for teams that want contact and deal management alongside a broader marketing, sales and customer-service ecosystem. Check which capabilities are included in the plan you actually need.

Five questions before you choose

1. Who needs access?

Count sales reps, managers and other users. Check paid-seat requirements and permissions.

2. What must be automated?

Write down your actual lead-routing, follow-up and approval steps, then verify them in the plan.

3. What already connects?

List your email, forms, marketing, accounting and support systems.

4. What is the full annual cost?

Include subscriptions, onboarding, data migration, training and any add-ons.

5. Can you leave later?

Check contact and deal export, historical activity and migration constraints.
